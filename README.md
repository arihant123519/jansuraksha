# JanSuraksha

**JanSuraksha** ("citizen safety") is a platform that lets citizens report vehicle and traffic-related misconduct directly to the police — and lets the police triage, track, and act on those reports efficiently.

## The problem

Today, reporting a rash driver, an illegally modified vehicle, or a repeat-offender number plate means a phone call or a station visit — no reference number, no status updates, and no easy way for it to be cross-checked against other complaints. On the police side, complaints pile up with no central view: no way to see where incidents are clustering, no way to spot the same vehicle showing up across multiple unrelated complaints, and no quick way to pull records for reporting.

JanSuraksha replaces that with a simple, trackable, real-time complaint system for both sides.

> 🚧 **Status:** This project is under active development. Features and workflows described below are being built out and may change.

## For citizens

- Report an incident in minutes, with photo/video evidence attached.
- Log in with just a mobile number and OTP — no lengthy signup.
- Track a complaint's status anytime using its complaint ID, even without logging in.
- See your own complaint history in one place.

## For police

- A single dashboard to view, filter, and update complaints as they come in.
- Live updates — new complaints and status changes appear instantly, no refreshing.
- A map and heatmap view to see where incidents are concentrated.
- Analytics to spot trends over time.
- Automatic flagging of vehicles/numbers that show up across multiple complaints, so patterns of repeat offenders don't go unnoticed.
- One-click export of complaint records for official reporting.
- Role-based access — officers, supervisors, and admins see and can act on what's relevant to them.

## Who it's for

Police departments and municipal safety teams looking for a lightweight way to digitize citizen complaints about road and vehicle safety, and citizens who want their reports to actually be tracked and acted on rather than disappearing into a phone call.
